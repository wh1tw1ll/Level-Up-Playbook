## Downtown Dova District — Signage Program

**Issued:** September 24, 2026

**Owner:** KozPure Development
**Owner's Rep:** Level Up Project Development

---

## 1. Project Overview

Downtown Dova is a mixed-use district development in Rancho Cordova, CA anchored by the Cordova Arena (7,500-seat indoor venue). The project team is seeking qualified design-build signage partners to develop, fabricate, and install a comprehensive signage program for the **district** (public realm, streetscape, district entry points, and wayfinding).

**This RFP covers district signage only.** Arena interior signage, exterior arena metal panels, arena LED displays, and arena entry canopies are being procured under separate scopes.

## 2. Scope of Work

The selected partner will provide design-build services for:

### A. Monumentation Signage
- District entry monument signs at key vehicular access points
- Primary and secondary monumentation identifying the Downtown Dova district
- Materials, finishes, and scale to align with district design guidelines and architectural vision established by Perkins & Will (Architect of Record)

### B. District Wayfinding Signage
- Vehicular wayfinding directing visitors to arena parking, district parking structures, and venue entries
- Pedestrian wayfinding throughout the district core — connecting arena, retail/entertainment, and public spaces
- Signage family with a consistent design language across all wayfinding elements
- Potential integration with branded trailblazer signage from regional corridors (Hwy 50, etc.)

### C. Program Development
- Site survey and inventory of all proposed sign locations
- Recommendations for sign types, quantities, and placement
- Integration with district branding and Perkins & Will architectural design standards
- Coordination with civil/landscape design for foundation and utility rough-ins
- Real-time cost feedback as design evolves (preferred engagement model per project team)

## 3. Engagement Model

The project team prefers a **Total Value Design Agreement** model (or similar early engagement approach):

- Partner engages early alongside the design team (Perkins & Will) during Schematic Design
- Partner provides real-time budget feedback as the district signage program develops
- Pre-construction fee range: **$60K–$90K** anticipated for this scope
- Pre-construction fee credited back if partner is awarded the fabrication/installation contract

Alternatively, traditional design-bid-build proposals will also be considered.

## 4. Submission Requirements

Interested firms shall provide:

1. **Company Overview** — Firm background, years in business, relevant experience with district/mixed-use signage programs
2. **Portfolio** — 3–5 examples of similar projects (district signage, wayfinding, monumentation)
3. **Project Approach** — Proposed process for design development, approvals, fabrication, and installation, including how you would collaborate with Perkins & Will
4. **Team** — Key personnel proposed with roles and relevant experience
5. **Preliminary Budget** — Rough order of magnitude for:
   - Design fees (signage program development)
   - Pre-construction services (if different from design)
   - Fabrication and installation (by sign type if possible)
6. **Schedule** — Proposed timeline from design through installation
7. **References** — 3 client references from comparable projects
8. **Capacity** — Current workload and availability for Q4 2026 / Q1 2027
