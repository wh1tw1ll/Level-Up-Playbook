# REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS
## Downtown Dova District — Signage Program
### Design-Build Services

**Issued:** September 24, 2026
**Project:** Downtown Dova (Rancho Cordova, CA)
**Owner:** KozPure Development

---

## 1. Project Overview

Downtown Dova is a mixed-use district development in Rancho Cordova, CA. The project team is seeking qualified design-build signage partners to develop, fabricate, and install a comprehensive signage program for the **district** (public realm, streetscape, and district entry points).

This RFP covers **district signage only**. Arena interior signage will be procured under a separate scope.

## 2. Scope of Work

The selected partner will provide design-build services for:

### A. Monumentation Signage
- District entry monument signs (locations TBD by site plan)
- Primary and secondary monumentation at key vehicular access points
- Materials, finishes, and scale to align with district design guidelines

### B. District Wayfinding Signage
- Vehicular wayfinding directing visitors to parking, venues, and district amenities
- Pedestrian wayfinding throughout the district core
- Signage family (consistent design language across all wayfinding elements)
- Potential integration with branded trailblazer signage from regional corridors

### C. Program Development
- Site survey and inventory of sign locations
- Recommendations for sign types, quantities, and placement
- Integration with district branding and architectural design standards
- Coordination with civil/landscape design for foundation and utility rough-ins

## 3. Submission Requirements

Interested firms shall provide:

1. **Company Overview** — Firm background, years in business, relevant project experience
2. **Portfolio** — Examples of similar district/mixed-use signage programs (3–5 projects)
3. **Project Approach** — Proposed process for design development, approvals, fabrication, and installation
4. **Team** — Key personnel proposed for this project with roles and experience
5. **Rough Order of Magnitude** — Preliminary budget ranges for design fees, fabrication, and installation
6. **Availability** — Current capacity and proposed timeline for design through installation
7. **References** — 3 client references from comparable projects

## 4. Evaluation Criteria

Proposals will be evaluated on:
- **50%** — Relevant experience and portfolio quality
- **25%** — Approach and understanding of district-scale signage
- **15%** — Team qualifications
- **10%** — Rough order of magnitude / value
